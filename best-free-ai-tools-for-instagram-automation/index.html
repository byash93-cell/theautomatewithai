Best Free AI Tools for Instagram Automation

Updated 16 October 2026 · by AutomateWithAI

Quick answer: A solid free stack is ChatGPT or Gemini for scripts and captions, CapCut and Canva for reels and graphics, Pexels for stock footage, Meta Business Suite for scheduling, and Zapier or Make for connecting apps. All of these have free plans as of October 2026, but free-plan limits change, so check each pricing page.

What should a free Instagram automation stack do?

A simple stack covers four jobs: ideas and scripts, visuals and video, scheduling and connecting apps so repetitive steps happen on their own. Every tool below offers a free plan or is free to use as of 16 October 2026. Free-plan limits change often, so check each provider's pricing page before you rely on one.

Which free AI tools write captions and scripts?

ChatGPT

OpenAI's ChatGPT has a free tier you can use to brainstorm reel ideas, draft hooks and scripts, rewrite captions and turn notes into lists. Free users get limited access to the most advanced models, and the limits are set by OpenAI. Check its facts before posting.

Google Gemini

Gemini is Google's AI assistant, free to use with a Google account. It is useful for scripts, caption variations and summarising research, and it works well alongside Google Docs and Sheets.

Which free tools make reels and graphics?

CapCut

CapCut is a video editor for phone, desktop and web with a free tier. It includes templates, auto captions, text-to-speech and easy trimming, which makes it a common choice for short-form video. Some effects and features require a paid plan.

Canva

Canva Free gives you templates for posts, stories, reel covers and simple videos, plus a free library of photos and graphics. Premium templates and some tools, like one-click background removal, are part of paid plans.

Pexels

Pexels offers stock photos and videos that are free to use under the Pexels license, including for commercial projects, without required attribution. It's a quick way to fill b-roll in faceless reels. Read the license for exceptions, such as not selling unaltered copies.

How can I schedule Instagram posts for free?

Meta Business Suite

Meta Business Suite is Meta's free tool for Facebook Pages and linked Instagram professional accounts. It lets you schedule posts and reels from a computer, view insights and manage messages in one inbox. Meta doesn't charge for it; you only pay if you choose to run ads. How far ahead you can schedule varies by account, so check the Planner's date picker.

Which free tools connect apps automatically?

Zapier

Zapier connects thousands of apps with "Zaps": when something happens in one app, an action runs in another. Its Free plan includes 100 tasks per month, and Make's comparison page notes the free plan is limited to two-step workflows. That's enough for simple automations, like saving new form sign-ups to a Google Sheet.

Make

Make (formerly Integromat) is a visual automation builder. Its Free plan includes 1,000 credits per month; most actions use one credit. It suits multi-step flows with routers and filters while you are learning.

Figures checked on 16 October 2026 from Zapier's help center and Make's pricing page. They can change at any time.

How do these tools fit together?

  1. Brainstorm a week of reel ideas and scripts in ChatGPT or Gemini.
  2. Record real app screens, add Pexels b-roll and edit in CapCut.
  3. Design covers and story graphics in Canva.
  4. Schedule the reels in Meta Business Suite.
  5. Use Zapier or Make for the back office, such as logging new email sign-ups or sending a welcome email.

If your reels feature a realistic AI person or voice, turn on Instagram's AI info label when you post.

What are the limits of free tools?

Free plans usually cap usage (messages, tasks, credits, exports), hold back premium templates or models, and can change without much notice. Start free, find out which tool you actually use every day, and only upgrade that one if the limit really gets in your way.
