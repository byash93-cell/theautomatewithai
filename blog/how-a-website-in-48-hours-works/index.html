How a small website can be built in about 48 hours

By AutomateWithAI · Updated 2026-10-27

Quick answer: On our services page, a custom website is $25 for 1 to 3 pages, mobile-friendly, with one revision round. Delivery is usually within 48 hours after the scope is agreed, not from your first message; bigger jobs take longer. A domain name is not included.

Step 1: send a brief

You fill in the order form on the services page: business name, purpose, pages, style, colours and whether your text and images are ready. It opens an email addressed to automatewithai@mail.grokbot.com.

Step 2: scope is confirmed, then you pay

We reply with a confirmed scope and a Gumroad payment link. Work starts after payment. The 48-hour clock starts once the scope is agreed.

Step 3: build, host and one revision

The site is a responsive static site. It is hosted free on Cloudflare Pages or Netlify's free plan (we set it up) or delivered as files. You get one revision round.

What is not included

  • A domain name (you buy it if you want one).
  • More than 3 pages, online shops and logins; those are bigger jobs.
  • Ongoing updates, unless you add the optional hosting and updates plan at $10 per month.

For a simple brochure site, yes. See the cost comparison for honest trade-offs.
