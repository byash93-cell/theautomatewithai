Does a small business website need a chatbot?

By AutomateWithAI · Updated 2026-10-27

Quick answer: A small business website needs a chatbot only if visitors keep asking the same questions (hours, prices, delivery, how to book) and you cannot answer them quickly. A simple FAQ chatbot handles those repeat questions; it is not a replacement for you on complex or sensitive ones.

When a chatbot helps

  • You get the same 10 to 20 questions again and again.
  • Visitors arrive outside your working hours.
  • Your answers are factual and do not change daily (opening times, pricing tiers, service areas).

When it does not

  • Most questions are unique or need a quote.
  • You handle sensitive topics such as health or legal advice.
  • You have very few visitors: a clear FAQ section on the page may be enough.

What a simple chatbot can do

Our website chatbot service is a FAQ-style widget for one website, built from up to 20 questions and answers you provide, with one revision round. It answers from that list. It is not a general assistant and it does not take payments.

A 10-minute test before you buy anything

Write down the last 20 messages customers sent you. If at least half are repeats, a chatbot is worth testing. If not, improve the FAQ section of your site first.
