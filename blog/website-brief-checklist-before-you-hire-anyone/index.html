Website brief checklist: what to prepare before you hire anyone

By AutomateWithAI · Updated 2026-10-27

Quick answer: A good website brief answers six things: what the site is for, which pages you need, what text and images you already have, two or three sites you like, your colours and logo, and your deadline. The clearer the brief, the fewer revisions you will need.

1. Purpose

One sentence: what should a visitor do after reading the site? Call, email, book or buy?

2. Pages

Most small businesses need Home, About or Services, and Contact. Keep to what you will keep up to date.

3. Text and images

Say what is ready. Real photos of your work almost always beat stock images. If text is not ready, say so, since writing it is extra work.

4. Style references

Send 2 to 3 links to sites you like and say what you like about them (layout, colours, tone).

5. Colours and logo

Share your logo and colours, or say you have none. We offer a logo and brand kit if you need one.

6. Deadline

Give a date. A clear limit prevents scope creep. Our order form asks for all of the above.

FAQ

How long should a brief be?

Half a page is enough if it covers purpose, pages, content, style and deadline.

Do I need to know technical terms?

No. Describe what you want in plain words and show examples.

What if my text is not ready?

Say so in the brief; it changes the scope.
