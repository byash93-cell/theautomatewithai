How to pick your first small business automation

By AutomateWithAI · Updated 2026-10-27

Quick answer: Automate one repeat task that happens at least weekly, follows clear rules, and takes noticeable time each run. Good first picks are saving form entries to a sheet, sending a standard confirmation email, or posting reminders. Start with one workflow on free plans before paying for anything.

Score your tasks

List your repeat tasks and give each a score from 1 to 3 for how often it happens, how clear the rules are and how much time it takes. Pick the highest total.

Three good first candidates

  • New form entry: copy it to a spreadsheet and send a confirmation.
  • Standard reply: a saved answer to a common question.
  • Weekly report: pull the same numbers into one place.

What to avoid at first

  • Anything that needs judgement or touches money without a human check.
  • Workflows with many exceptions.
  • Anything that could break platform rules, such as bulk messaging.
