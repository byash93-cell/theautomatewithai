Cheap AI Website for Small Business: $25, Honest Scope

Updated 22 October 2026 · by AutomateWithAI

Quick answer: For $25 one-time, we design and build a 1 to 3 page responsive website from your idea using AI tools, with 1 revision round, hosted free on Cloudflare Pages or Netlify's free plan (we set it up), or delivered as files. A domain isn't included. Compared with Wix's free plan there are no builder ads, but there's no drag-and-drop editor. Delivery is usually within 48 hours after the scope is agreed.

What's included for $25?

  • 1 to 3 pages, mobile-friendly (responsive) design
  • Built from your idea, text and photos (we can draft placeholder text)
  • Hosted free on Cloudflare Pages or Netlify's free plan (we set it up), or delivered as files
  • 1 revision round
  • Optional: hosting and updates for $10/month

What's not included?

  • A domain name (you buy it if you want one)
  • Online shop, payments, bookings systems, logins or databases
  • Unlimited changes: 1 revision round is included
  • Guaranteed rankings or traffic: nobody can honestly promise those

Hosting note: Netlify says its Free plan can be used to "deploy commercial projects" (Netlify, pricing); if a free site goes over its monthly limits it is suspended until the month resets or you upgrade. Cloudflare's Self-Serve Subscription Agreement has no commercial-use restriction for the Free plan, and its Pages limits include 500 builds a month. We don't use GitHub Pages for client sites, because GitHub says it isn't for running an online business (GitHub).

Frequently asked questions

How much does the AI website cost?

$25 one-time for 1 to 3 responsive pages with 1 revision round. A domain is not included. Optional hosting and updates cost $10 a month.

What is not included in the $25 website?

No domain name, no online shop or payments, no logins or databases, no ongoing edits after the included revision unless you add the $10 a month hosting and updates option.

Is a $25 website better than Wix's free plan?

It depends. Wix's free plan has unlimited pages and a drag-and-drop editor, but it shows Wix ads on every page and uses a wixsite.com address. The $25 site is built for you with no builder ads, but you edit it as code or ask us to.

Does Squarespace have a free plan?

No. Squarespace says it doesn't offer a free plan; every site starts with a 14-day free trial, and the site stays private until you upgrade to a paid plan.
