How to Automate Instagram Posting for Free (Schedule Reels with Meta Business Suite)

Updated 16 October 2026 · by AutomateWithAI

Quick answer: Use Meta's free tools. Connect your Instagram professional account to a Facebook Page, open Meta Business Suite, create a post or reel, and choose Schedule instead of Publish. You can also schedule from the Instagram app itself: Meta says public accounts can schedule reels, photos and carousels up to 30 days ahead without a subscription. Batch a week of reels in one sitting and let them post on their own.

What are the free ways to schedule Instagram posts?

  • Meta Business Suite (web and mobile app): Meta's free tool for Facebook Pages and linked Instagram professional accounts. You can schedule feed posts, reels and stories, see the queue in the Planner calendar, and manage messages and insights in one place. Meta only charges if you run ads.
  • The Instagram app: according to Meta's Help Center, public accounts can schedule reels, photos and carousel posts from the app, up to 30 days ahead without a subscription. With an eligible Meta One plan (Advanced, Expert or Max) it's up to a year.

How do I set up Meta Business Suite for Instagram?

  1. Switch your Instagram account to a professional account (Creator or Business) in the app's settings.
  2. Connect it to a Facebook Page you manage.
  3. Sign in at business.facebook.com or open the Meta Business Suite app and confirm both accounts appear.

How do I schedule a reel step by step?

  1. In Meta Business Suite, choose Create reel (or Create post for feed posts).
  2. Select Instagram as the placement (you can add the Facebook Page too).
  3. Upload your edited vertical video, add the caption, cover and hashtags.
  4. Open the scheduling options, pick the date and time, and click Schedule.
  5. Check it in the Planner or Content tab, where you can reschedule or edit it.

Menu names can change slightly between versions and between web and app.

How far ahead can I schedule?

Meta's Help Center states 30 days ahead from the Instagram app for public accounts without a subscription (source). In Meta Business Suite the window varies by account: Meta's help pages have cited a longer window, but many admins report the Planner date picker stopping at about 29–30 days. The practical rule: plan around four weeks and check your own date picker.

What are the limits?

  • Your account must be a professional account linked to a Facebook Page for Meta Business Suite.
  • Some music from the in-app library may not be available when scheduling from a computer. Add licensed audio in your editor, or post trending-audio reels from the app.
  • Scheduling doesn't reply for you: be around for the first comments after a reel goes live.
  • If a reel features a realistic AI person or voice, add the AI label; check that the option is available where you schedule.

A free weekly batching workflow

  1. Write 7 ideas and scripts with free ChatGPT (see our prompts guide).
  2. Record real screens and edit in CapCut; make covers in Canva.
  3. Schedule all 7 in Meta Business Suite in one sitting, at times your Insights show your audience is active.
  4. Review Insights after a week and keep what works.

Frequently asked questions
