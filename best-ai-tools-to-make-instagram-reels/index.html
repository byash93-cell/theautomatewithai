Best AI Tools to Make Instagram Reels (Free and Paid, Honest Picks)

Updated 16 October 2026 · by AutomateWithAI

Quick answer: For most creators: ChatGPT (free) for hooks and scripts, CapCut (free) to edit with auto captions, Pexels for free B-roll, and a voice tool. Use VoiceStudio (free, local) or ElevenLabs, whose free plan is non-commercial and needs attribution. Descript and OpusClip have free plans too, but their free exports carry a watermark. Free-tier facts below were checked on 17 October 2026.

How we picked

We grouped tools by the job they do in a reel, and for each free tier we checked the limits that actually matter: watermarks, monthly credits and whether you may use the output commercially. Pricing changes often, so always confirm on the provider's pricing page.

Scripting and hooks

ChatGPT (free tier)

Fast for hooks, 25-second scripts and shot lists. Free users have usage limits set by OpenAI. Use our copy-paste reel prompts.

Voiceover

VoiceStudio (free, open source, local)

Runs on your own computer (GPU optional), with cloning, voice design and dubbing. See our VoiceStudio guide.

ElevenLabs (free and paid)

Free plan: 10,000 credits a month, no card needed. But per ElevenLabs' help center, the free plan has no commercial licence, and public content must credit ElevenLabs ("elevenlabs.io" or "11.ai" in the title). Paid plans start at $6/month (Starter) and include a commercial licence. If your page sells anything, that matters.

Editing and captions

CapCut (free tier)

Free editor on mobile, web and PC with split, text templates, auto captions and text-to-speech. Some effects need CapCut Pro to export. Pair it with the notification-banner hook technique.

Descript (free and paid)

Edit video by editing the transcript. Its pricing page lists the Free plan as 1 media hour a month, 100 one-time AI credits, and 720p exports with a watermark. The Hobbyist tier removes the watermark and exports 1080p.

Clipping long videos into reels

OpusClip (free and paid)

Turns a long video into short clips with AI captions and 9:16 reframing. Its Free plan gives 60 processing minutes a month (about an hour of source video), but free clips have a watermark and can only be exported for 3 days. Paid plans start at $15/month (Starter).

B-roll and visuals

Pexels (free)

Free stock photos and videos under the Pexels licence, including commercial use without required attribution (with some restrictions). For real-screen tutorials, record your own screen. It's more trustworthy than stock.

Canva (free tier)

Covers, banners and simple graphics; some templates and tools are Pro-only.

Our honest stack by budget

  • $0: ChatGPT + CapCut + Pexels + VoiceStudio or your own voice.
  • Small budget: add a paid voice plan (for a commercial licence) or a watermark-free editor, but only once a free tool's limit really blocks you.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best free AI tool to make Instagram reels?

For most people, CapCut's free editor (with auto captions) combined with free ChatGPT for scripts and Pexels for B-roll covers the whole reel at no cost.

Can I use ElevenLabs' free plan for Instagram reels?

Only non-commercially and with attribution. ElevenLabs says free-plan content cannot be used commercially and must credit ElevenLabs in the title; paid plans include a commercial licence.

Does Descript's free plan add a watermark?

Descript's pricing page lists Free plan exports at 720p with a watermark; paid plans export watermark-free.

Is OpusClip free?

It has a free plan with 60 processing minutes a month, but free clips carry a watermark and can only be exported for 3 days.
