How to Start a Faceless AI Instagram Page (Free Tools)

Updated 16 October 2026 · by AutomateWithAI

Quick answer: Pick one niche, switch to a professional Instagram account, write scripts with a free AI assistant like ChatGPT or Gemini, and edit real screen recordings and stock clips in CapCut. Then schedule reels in Meta Business Suite, set up saved replies for DMs, label realistic AI people with Instagram's AI info label, and review Insights weekly.

What is a faceless AI Instagram page?

A faceless page is an Instagram account where the creator never has to appear on camera. The content is built from screen recordings, stock footage, text on screen, voiceovers, or an AI character. AI tools help with the slow parts: brainstorming ideas, writing scripts, generating voiceovers and cutting videos. You still choose the topics, check the facts and decide what gets posted. That human review is what separates a useful page from a spammy one.

This guide walks through the whole setup using tools that have free plans. Nothing here needs coding, and you can do every step from a laptop and a phone.

How do I choose a niche for a faceless AI page?

Pick one topic you can make at least 30 posts about without running out of ideas. Good faceless niches are ones where the screen does the talking: AI tool tutorials, productivity tips, coding snippets, personal finance basics, study hacks, recipes, travel facts or motivational quotes. Ask yourself three questions:

  • Can I show it without my face? Tutorials and screen recordings work well; personal vlogs do not.
  • Who exactly is it for? "Students who want to study faster with AI" is better than "everyone".
  • Can I stay on topic? Instagram learns who to show your reels to from what you post. A page that jumps between topics confuses that signal.

Write a one-line promise for your page, for example: "Free AI tools that save you an hour a day, shown on real screens." Use it in your bio and check every post idea against it.

How do I set up the Instagram account?

  1. Create a new account with a clear username that matches your niche, and use a simple logo or icon as the profile photo.
  2. Switch to a professional account (Creator or Business) in Settings. This unlocks insights, scheduling tools and extra message features.
  3. Write a bio that states who the page is for and what they get, in one or two short lines.
  4. Link the account to a Facebook Page if you want to schedule from Meta Business Suite on a computer.
  5. Turn on two-factor authentication so you don't lose the account.

Warm the account up like a normal person would: browse, follow a few accounts in your niche and post a few times before doing anything in bulk. Sudden bursts of follows or comments from a brand-new account can trigger Instagram's automated checks.

How do I write reel scripts with AI?

Free assistants like ChatGPT or Google Gemini can draft scripts quickly. A good short reel script has three parts: a hook in the first one to two seconds, a fast demonstration or list of steps, and a clear ending such as "save this for later". A simple prompt that works:

"Write a 30-second Instagram reel script for [audience] about [topic]. Start with a hook under 10 words that shows the result first. Then give 3 short steps. End with a call to save the post. Use plain words."

Always read the script back and fix anything that is wrong or exaggerated. AI assistants can state outdated or incorrect facts confidently, so check tool names, prices and features yourself before you film.

Which free tools can I use to make the videos?

  • CapCut is a popular video editor with a free tier, templates, auto captions and text-to-speech. It is the easiest way to cut screen recordings and add zooms, text and music.
  • MoneyPrinterTurbo is an open-source project on GitHub that turns a topic or script into a short video by combining an AI-written script, stock clips, voiceover, subtitles and music. It runs on your own computer and needs some setup, so it suits people comfortable following install instructions.
  • Pexels offers free stock photos and videos you can use in your edits.
  • Screen recorders such as OBS Studio (free and open source) let you record real app screens. Real screens build trust far better than animated mock-ups.

Export vertically at 1080×1920 so your reels look sharp on phones.

How should I write captions and hashtags?

Keep the first line of the caption as strong as the video hook, because it is all people see before tapping "more". Add one or two lines of useful context, a question that invites comments, and a handful of specific hashtags that match your niche. Turn on captions inside the video too: many people watch reels with the sound off.

Do I need to label AI-generated people on Instagram?

Yes. If your reels use a realistic AI person, AI voice or AI-generated video that looks real, Meta requires you to disclose it using Instagram's AI info label, and Meta may apply the label itself when it detects AI content. Leaving it off can lead to penalties on your content. Turn the label on when you post from the app, and it also helps to say "Made with AI" in the caption. Being upfront keeps your audience's trust.

How do I schedule posts with Meta Business Suite?

Meta Business Suite is Meta's free tool for managing Facebook Pages and linked Instagram accounts. From a computer you can open the Planner, create a reel or post, pick Instagram, add your caption and choose a date and time. Instagram's own app can also schedule posts and reels for professional accounts. Batch-film several reels in one session, then schedule them across the week so you post consistently even on busy days. How far ahead you can schedule depends on your account and Meta's current rules, so check the date picker in your Planner.

How do I set up DM auto-replies for free?

Instagram's saved replies let professional accounts store answers to common questions and insert them with a short keyword, which makes answering DMs much faster. Meta Business Suite's inbox also offers automated responses such as instant replies and frequently asked questions for some accounts. Write replies for the questions you get most: "Where's the link?", "Is it free?", "What tool is this?". Keep them friendly and human, and still read your messages, since automated replies can't handle everything.

What should I put in the link in bio?

Instagram lets you add links to your profile. Put your most important destination first: a free resource, your website, or your product page. Add a tracking tag such as ?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=bio to your website link so your analytics show which visitors came from Instagram.

How do I measure whether it's working?

Open Insights in the Instagram app or Meta Business Suite once a week. Look at:

  • Reach and views, to see how many people saw each reel.
  • Average watch time and retention, to see whether your hook holds people.
  • Saves, shares and comments, which show the content was useful.
  • Profile visits and link taps, which show interest in what you offer.

Compare your best and worst reels, make more of what works and change one thing at a time. Growth is uneven and depends on your niche, consistency and quality, so judge trends over weeks, not single posts.

Frequently asked questions

Can I run an Instagram page without showing my face?

Yes. Many pages use screen recordings, stock footage, on-screen text, voiceovers or AI characters instead of a person on camera.

Which free tools do I need to start a faceless AI Instagram page?

A free AI assistant such as ChatGPT or Gemini for scripts, CapCut for editing, Pexels for stock footage, a screen recorder such as OBS Studio, and Meta Business Suite for scheduling. Free-plan limits can change.

Do I have to label AI-generated people on Instagram?

Yes. Meta requires you to use Instagram's AI info label for realistic AI-generated people, voices or video, and adding 'Made with AI' to the caption helps viewers too.

How often should a new faceless page post?

Post on a schedule you can keep. Consistent posting on one topic helps Instagram learn who to show your reels to; batch-creating and scheduling makes this easier.

Will a faceless AI page make money?

There is no guarantee. Results depend on your niche, effort, content quality and consistency.
