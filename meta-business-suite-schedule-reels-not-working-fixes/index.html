Meta Business Suite Reel Scheduling Not Working? Fixes and Limits

Updated 22 October 2026 · by AutomateWithAI

Quick answer: In Meta Business Suite go to Content → Posts & reels → Scheduled, click … on the reel and choose Manage post to edit, reschedule, move to drafts or delete it. Scheduling times use your current time zone. If a reel didn't publish, check the Scheduled tab for an error and use Publish now. If an edit won't save, move the reel to drafts or recreate it. Every fix below is sourced from Meta's Help Center.

Quick check: what does Meta actually document?

Everything below comes from Meta's Help Center: Create and manage reels in Meta Business Suite, Schedule a post and manage scheduled posts, Troubleshoot errors creating, scheduling, or publishing posts, Best practices for using the Planner and Reschedule posts and stories on mobile. Where Meta doesn't say something, we say so instead of guessing. Menus change, so match the names to what you see on screen.

How do I edit a scheduled reel?

On desktop: Content → Posts & reels tab → Scheduled → click … on the reel → Manage post. Meta lists these options, which depend on the reel's status: Edit reel, Reschedule post, Change date, Move to drafts, Delete reel, Change remix and audio use and Add collaborator (source).

On the mobile app: Content → dropdown → Scheduled → Options → Reschedule post. From that same menu you can also edit the details, duplicate or delete the content (source).

My caption edits on a scheduled reel didn't save. What now?

Meta's reels page lists Edit reel but does not say which fields it lets you change on a scheduled reel. Note also that you can't edit a reel from a video if posting to Instagram (source); Meta's in-app video editing step (audio, aspect ratio, trimming) is for Facebook only.

What to try, in order:

  1. Make the edit, save, then reload the Scheduled tab and open the reel again to confirm the change stuck.
  2. Try the same edit in the other place (desktop vs. the mobile app's Options menu).
  3. If it still won't save, use Move to drafts, fix the caption in the draft, and schedule it again.
  4. Last resort: Delete reel and create it again with the right caption. Do this well before the scheduled time.

Remember that words spoken or shown in the video can't be changed by editing the caption, so re-export the video if those are wrong.

Why is my reel scheduled for the wrong time?

Meta says: “All times for scheduling correspond to your current time zone.” Its scheduling page also says posts can be scheduled between 20 minutes and 29 days away (source). That page is about Page posts; the reels page doesn't state a separate window. If you work across time zones, or someone else schedules for you from another country, double-check the time shown before clicking Schedule, and recheck it in the Planner afterwards.

How do I set the reel's cover image?

When creating a reel from a video on desktop, Meta's steps say: under Thumbnail, choose a thumbnail or upload an image (source). Set it while creating the reel. Meta doesn't document changing the thumbnail of an already-scheduled reel, so if Edit reel doesn't offer it, move the reel to drafts or recreate it.

Why is my scheduled post missing or not published?

Meta lists three symptoms: an error when scheduling, a post set for both Facebook and Instagram not publishing to both, and a scheduled post not publishing on time (source). The causes it gives:

  • No access to publish: ask the business portfolio owner to give you access to the Page or Instagram account.
  • Unresolved Instagram account issues: open the Instagram app and follow any pop-up notification, then try again in Business Suite.
  • Hacked account: if you can't log in, follow Instagram's hacked-account steps.
  • System error: go to Content → Scheduled, look for an error message, open the failed post and click Publish now.

Also check you're looking at the right tab: Meta sorts reels into Published, Scheduled, Drafts, Expiring and Expired (source). To create a reel at all you need full control or content permission for the Page or Instagram account.

How do I see everything in the Planner?

The Planner shows scheduled posts, stories, reels and ads in a weekly or monthly view (source). Use it to spot gaps, duplicates and days with too many reels. Then fix individual items from the Content tab using the steps above.

Limits worth knowing

  • You can't create a reel from photos when posting to Instagram (source).
  • Bulk-uploaded reels can only go to your Facebook Page, not your Instagram account (source).
  • Reels from business or creator Pages can only be posted publicly, per Meta’s mobile steps (source).
