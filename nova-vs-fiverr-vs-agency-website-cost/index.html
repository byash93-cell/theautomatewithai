Nova vs Fiverr vs Agency: Small Website Cost Compared

Updated 22 October 2026 · by AutomateWithAI

Quick answer: For a simple 1 to 3 page site, Nova (our AI character) charges $25 one-time with 1 revision, usually within 48 hours after the scope is agreed. A $25 Fiverr gig costs about $29.88 once Fiverr's 5.5% fee and $3.50 small-order fee are added, with delivery time and revisions set by the freelancer. Agencies quote per project and do far bigger work; there's no official public price.

Worked example: a $25 order

  • Nova: $25 total. No fee is added at checkout.
  • Fiverr $25 gig: $25 + 5.5% ($1.38) + $3.50 small-order fee = $29.88, before any taxes (Fiverr).
  • Agency: usually not offered at this size; ask for a written quote.

When each option makes sense

  • Nova: you want a small, clean information site quickly, with a fixed price and clear scope. No shop, logins or databases.
  • Fiverr: you want to choose a specific freelancer and style, and compare many offers. On Fiverr you have 3 days after delivery to accept or request revisions before the order completes automatically (Fiverr).
  • Agency: you need strategy, custom features, e-commerce or ongoing support, and have the budget for it.

What Nova's $25 site includes

Frequently asked questions

How much does a small website cost with Nova?

$25 one-time for 1 to 3 responsive pages with 1 revision round. A domain is not included. Delivery is usually within 48 hours after the scope is agreed by email.

What fees does Fiverr add for buyers?

Fiverr's Help Center says it adds a service fee of 5.5% of the purchase price, plus $3.50 on orders under $200. On a $25 gig that comes to about $29.88 before any taxes.

How many revisions do Fiverr gigs include?

Each freelancer sets a revision count per package, and Fiverr requires at least one revision option per package.

What does a web agency charge?

Agencies quote per project and there is no official public price list. For context, the US Bureau of Labor Statistics lists 2025 median pay for web developers and digital designers at $47.85 per hour.
