Free ChatGPT Prompts for Instagram Captions

Updated 22 October 2026 · by AutomateWithAI

Quick answer: Tell ChatGPT your niche, audience, what the post shows and the one takeaway, then ask for several short captions with a hook in the first line. Add a small set of specific hashtags, alt text and one honest call to action. The 7 prompts below work on ChatGPT's free tier. Always edit the result into your own voice and check facts before posting.

How should I use these prompts?

Replace the [BRACKETS] with your details. Ask for several options, pick one, and edit it until it sounds like you. Free ChatGPT has usage limits set by OpenAI, and AI can state wrong facts confidently, so check anything factual before you post. Instagram captions can be up to 2,200 characters with up to 30 hashtags, but shorter and specific usually reads better on a phone.

Prompt 1: 10 caption options with a strong first line

Use this for any post when you know what it shows.

You write Instagram captions for [NICHE] aimed at [WHO]. The post shows: [WHAT THE POST SHOWS]. The one takeaway: [TAKEAWAY]. Write 10 captions under 300 characters. Rule: the first line must work on its own as a hook (under 10 words), because only the first line or two show before "more". Tone: [FRIENDLY / DIRECT / FUNNY]. No hype, no income claims, max 1 emoji per line.

Prompt 2: a long, save-worthy caption

For carousels and tutorials where the caption carries the steps.

Write a long Instagram caption (150-250 words) for a post about [TOPIC]. Structure: hook line, blank line, 3-5 numbered steps with one short sentence each, one line on a common mistake, then "Save this so you can find it later." Use short lines and line breaks so it reads well on a phone. Only include steps I give you: [STEPS].

Prompt 3: rewrite in my voice

Stops captions sounding like every other AI caption.

Here are 3 captions I wrote myself: [PASTE 3 OF YOUR OLD CAPTIONS] Describe my writing style in 5 bullet points. Then rewrite this draft in that style, keeping the meaning: [PASTE DRAFT] Remove words like "unlock", "game-changer", "dive in" and "elevate".

Prompt 4: relevant hashtags

Asks for a small, specific set instead of 30 generic tags.

Suggest 8 hashtags for an Instagram post about [TOPIC] for [WHO]. Mix: 3 broad, 3 niche, 2 very specific. Explain in 5 words why each fits. Avoid banned-sounding, spammy or unrelated tags. I will check each tag on Instagram before using it.

Prompt 5: alt text

Instagram lets you add alt text to images, which helps people using screen readers.

Write alt text (under 125 characters) for an Instagram image that shows: [DESCRIBE THE IMAGE]. Describe what is visible, plainly. No hashtags, no "image of".

Prompt 6: one honest call to action

Gives a clear next step without fake urgency.

Give me 6 one-line calls to action for the end of an Instagram caption about [TOPIC]. Goals: 2 for saves, 2 for shares to a friend, 2 for "link in bio" to [WHAT IS AT THE LINK]. No fake urgency, no "last chance", no promises of results.

Prompt 7: caption checker

Run this before posting.

Review this Instagram caption as a strict editor. 1) Would the first line stop someone scrolling? Give a stronger version. 2) Cut every unnecessary word. 3) Flag any claim that may be false, exaggerated or needs a source. 4) Check it is under 2,200 characters and has no more than 30 hashtags. Caption: [PASTE CAPTION]

Frequently asked questions

What is a good ChatGPT prompt for Instagram captions?

Tell ChatGPT your niche, who the post is for, what the post shows and the one takeaway, then ask for several short options with a strong first line. Prompt 1 on this page is a ready template.

How long can an Instagram caption be?

Instagram captions can be up to 2,200 characters, and a post can have up to 30 hashtags. Only the first line or two show before "more", so put the hook first.

Can I use free ChatGPT for Instagram captions?

Yes. The free tier handles captions, hashtags and alt text. Free users have usage limits set by OpenAI, and you should edit and fact-check every caption.

Should I post ChatGPT captions as they are?

No. Rewrite them in your own voice, remove anything you can't back up, and check facts and links before posting.
